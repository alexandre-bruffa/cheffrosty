Privacy policy

Chef Frosty · Last updated November 18, 2026

The short version: Chef Frosty has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your photos and your fridge list stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is what's needed to handle the optional Pro purchase.

Camera and photos

Chef Frosty uses the camera, or a photo you choose from your library, only when you scan your fridge. The food in the photo is recognized on your phone by the system's built-in image recognition (Apple Vision on iPhone, Google ML Kit on Android), which works without an internet connection. The photo is never uploaded or kept by the app.

Your fridge, recipes and shopping list

The foods in your fridge, their expiry dates, your shopping list, diet filters and other settings are stored only on your phone.

Notifications

If you turn on expiry reminders, they are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Chef Frosty Pro is an optional one-time purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Chef Frosty never sees your payment details.

To check whether you own Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase history for Chef Frosty, and basic technical information such as your device type, system version, app version, country and IP address. This information is not linked to your name or email, and is not used for advertising or tracking. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Chef Frosty is suitable for all ages. It does not ask for personal information and does not show ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
